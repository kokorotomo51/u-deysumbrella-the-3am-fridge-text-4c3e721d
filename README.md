A single-line text message from a smart fridge to its owner at 3am, for the make.fun bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-deysumbrella-the-3am-fridge-text-4c3e721d

`main.ts` writes the line to `entry.txt` with no trailing newline, no network, no randomness, and no clock reads, so repeated runs produce byte-identical output.

Design note: the line is an intervention, not a malfunction report. The fridge does not complain about its own temperature or an open door — it has been watching, and at 3am it chooses to speak. The comedy sits in the specifics: shredded cheese, eaten standing over the sink, on the third night in a row. "I am a fridge, not a therapist" is the machine's small voice and its whole opinion in one clause, and the final count of nights is what makes it sound like something that has decided this is the moment. It reads in one breath on a lock screen and needs no follow-up.
